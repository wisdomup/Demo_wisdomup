e-Manuals

Quick-start guides for every product.

Pairing, charging and care for each WisdomUp product family. Need the full PDF manual? Email support@wisdomup.pk with your model name.

Pair

  1. Open the case and take the earbuds out — they switch on and enter pairing mode.
  2. Select your WisdomUp TS model in your phone's Bluetooth settings.
  3. After the first time, they reconnect when you open the case.

Charge

  1. Put the earbuds back in the case and close the lid to charge them.
  2. Charge the case with a USB-C cable.

Fit

  1. Try the ear-tip sizes in the box — a good seal improves bass and, on the TS-11ANC, noise cancelling.
  2. Model-specific touch controls are printed in the manual in the box.

Pair

  1. Open the charging case — the earbuds switch on and enter pairing mode.
  2. Select your WisdomUp OS model in your phone's Bluetooth settings.

Wear

  1. Place the earbud so the speaker sits just outside your ear canal.
  2. Adjust the angle until the sound points into your ear.

Charge

  1. Return the buds to the case and close the lid; charge the case with a USB-C cable.

Pair

  1. Hold the power button until the light flashes.
  2. Select the WisdomUp neckband in your Bluetooth settings.

Use

  1. Use the inline buttons for volume, tracks and calls.

Charge

  1. Charge with the included cable until the light changes (about 2 hours).

Wireless models

  1. Hold the power button until the light flashes, then select the model in your Bluetooth settings.
  2. On the TDE-18, switch active noise cancelling on or off with its ANC control.

Wired and gaming models

  1. Plug the 3.5mm or USB-C plug into your phone, laptop or console.
  2. On RGB gaming headsets, connect the USB lead to power the lights.

Set up

  1. Plug the receiver into your phone, laptop or tablet.
  2. Switch the transmitter on — it pairs with the receiver automatically.
  3. Open your camera or recording app.

Record

  1. Clip the mic 15–20 cm below your chin.
  2. The receiver lets your phone charge while you record.
  3. Read our clean audio guide for more tips.

Pair

  1. Hold the power button to switch on, then select the YX model in your Bluetooth settings.
  2. For stereo, pair two identical TWS-capable speakers using the TWS button (see the manual in the box).

Charge

  1. Charge fully before first use; playtime is measured at 50% volume.

Wireless chargers

  1. Connect with the included cable and a fast-charging adapter.
  2. On the CJ-46, place your phone, earbuds and watch on their pads.
  3. Remove thick or metal cases if charging is slow.

Power banks

  1. Charge the power bank fully before first use.
  2. Use the built-in cables, or the magnetic pad on the CDB-18.

Use

  1. Push the charger firmly into your car's 12–24V socket.
  2. Connect your device with a cable rated for fast charging.
  3. On CZ models, tune your car radio to the frequency shown and pair your phone by Bluetooth.

Care

  1. Unplug before starting the engine if your car's socket stays powered.
  2. Read charging at home and on the road.

Charge

  1. Charge fully before first use (about 1.5–3 hours, depending on the model).

Use

  1. Hold the power button to unlock models with a travel lock.
  2. Clip on a guide comb (LFJ models) for an even length.

Clean

  1. Brush hair from the blades after each use; IPX6 shavers can be rinsed under the tap.
