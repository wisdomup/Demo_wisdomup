e-Manuals

Quick-start guides for every product.

Pairing, charging and care for each WisdomUp product family. Need the full PDF manual? Email support@wisdomup.pk with your model name.

Pair

  1. Open the charging case — the earbuds turn on and enter pairing mode.
  2. On your phone, open Bluetooth settings and select your WisdomUp OS model.
  3. After the first time, they reconnect as soon as you open the case.

Charge

  1. Put the earbuds in the case and close the lid to charge them.
  2. Charge the case with the included Type-C cable.
  3. The case lights show charging status.

Wear

  1. Hook the ear loop over the top of your ear so the speaker sits just outside your ear canal.
  2. Adjust the angle until the sound points into your ear.

Pair

  1. Open the case and take the earbuds out.
  2. Select your WisdomUp TS model in your phone's Bluetooth settings.
  3. To pair a new phone, put the buds back in the case, then reopen it with the old phone's Bluetooth off.

Controls

  1. Touch controls handle play, pause, calls and voice assistant.
  2. On ANC models (TS-10), use the touch control to switch noise cancelling on and off.
  3. Model-specific gestures are printed in the manual in the box.

Fit

  1. Try the different ear-tip sizes in the box — a good seal improves bass and noise cancelling.

Pair

  1. Hold the power button until the light flashes.
  2. Select the WisdomUp neckband in your Bluetooth settings.

Use

  1. Snap the magnetic buds together when you're not listening.
  2. Use the inline buttons for volume, tracks and calls.

Charge

  1. Charge with the included cable until the light changes.

Set up

  1. Plug the receiver into your phone's Type-C or Lightning port.
  2. Switch the transmitter on — it pairs with the receiver automatically.
  3. Open your camera or recording app.

Record

  1. Clip the mic 15–20 cm below your chin, pointing at your mouth.
  2. On the MKF-02, use noise reduction for outdoor scenes.
  3. Read our clean audio guide for more tips.

Pair

  1. Hold the power button to switch on.
  2. Select Thunder Pro in your Bluetooth settings.

Use

  1. Use the light button to change the RGB light modes.
  2. Adjust the custom EQ to suit the room.
  3. Rated IPX6 — splash resistant, but don't submerge it.

Charge

  1. Charge with the included cable before your first party.

Set up

  1. Connect the charger with the included cable and a suitable power adapter.
  2. On the CJ-45, place your phone in the centre, your earbuds on the base and your watch on the holder.
  3. Remove thick or metal cases if charging is slow.

Use

  1. Push the charger firmly into your car's 12V socket.
  2. Connect your device with a cable rated for fast charging.
  3. The CC-16 display shows the voltage while it works.

Care

  1. Unplug before starting the engine if your car's socket stays powered.
  2. Read charging at home and on the road.
