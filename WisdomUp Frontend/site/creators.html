Program terms
  1. Joining is free. You need a Pakistani WhatsApp number and, for payouts, a JazzCash, EasyPaisa or bank account in your name.
  2. Commission is paid on the items in an order after your followers’ discount — not on delivery, gift wrap, or orders that are cancelled or returned.
  3. Commission is approved 7 days after delivery and paid monthly once your approved balance reaches Rs.1,000.
  4. A link counts for 30 days after a click; the last creator link clicked wins. A code typed at checkout counts too.
  5. Orders from your own number never earn commission. Fake or repeated orders, spam, and paid ads that use the WisdomUp name are not allowed.
  6. Mark posts that use your code as #ad or #affiliate, and never script false claims about a product.
  7. We may pause a code that breaks these terms; commission from orders that break them is cancelled.
  8. We may change rates with 30 days’ notice on your dashboard. Commission you have already earned is never reduced.
