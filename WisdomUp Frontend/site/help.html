Orders & delivery

Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days anywhere in Pakistan. Express delivery arrives in 1–2 working days. See the Shipping Policy.
Orders above Rs.10,000 ship free. Below that, the delivery charge is shown at checkout before you pay.

Returns & exchanges

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee from the date of delivery. Products must be in original condition and packaging. See the Exchange & Refund Policy.

Warranty

Every WisdomUp product has at least a 6-month warranty, and premium products carry up to 2 years. See the Warranty Policy.
