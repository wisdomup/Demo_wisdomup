What's covered

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use — for example, a bud that stops charging, a dead speaker driver, a charger that stops working, or a battery that fails early.

What's not covered

  • Physical damage, such as drops, cracks or crushed parts.
  • Liquid damage beyond the product's water-resistance rating.
  • Damage from misuse, unsuitable chargers or unauthorised repairs.
  • Normal wear, such as scratches or worn ear tips, and lost parts.

How to make a claim

  1. Contact support with your order number via the Help Center, email or phone.
  2. Describe the problem — a short video helps us diagnose it faster.
  3. If it's covered, we arrange a repair or replacement.

Buy genuine

The warranty applies to products bought from WisdomUp or an authorised retailer. See Where to Buy.

Bulk and corporate orders

Every unit in a bulk or corporate order carries the standard WisdomUp warranty, and partners get a dedicated contact for claims.
