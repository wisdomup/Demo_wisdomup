WisdomUp questions, answered

Is WisdomUp a Pakistani brand?
WisdomUp is an international tech-accessories brand sold in 50+ countries, with local stock, delivery, payments and customer support in Pakistan.
Which WisdomUp earbuds have noise cancelling?
The TS-11ANC earbuds have active noise cancelling and Bluetooth 6.0. For over-ear noise cancelling, choose the TDE-18 ANC wireless headphones with about 40 hours of play.
Which charger and cable do I need for my phone?
Most new phones charge fastest with a 20W PD USB-C charger and a USB-C cable (USB-C to Lightning for iPhone 14 and older). WisdomUp chargers come with the cable included, with an EU 2-pin or UK 3-pin plug.
Do WisdomUp products work with iPhone and Android?
Yes. All WisdomUp Bluetooth earbuds, neckbands, speakers and mics pair with iPhone, Android, Windows and macOS.
Can I order in bulk or for my company?
Yes. The WisdomUp Bulk Desk handles distributor, wholesale and corporate orders with dedicated bulk pricing.
