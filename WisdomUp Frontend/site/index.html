Wireless earbuds price in Pakistan

WisdomUp earbuds come in two families. The OS Open Stereo series (OS-1 to OS-5) sits outside the ear for all-day comfort and full awareness of traffic and conversation, with up to 40+ hours of battery on the OS-4. The TS True Wireless series (TS-2 to TS-11) brings ANC, ENC quad-mic calling, low-latency gaming modes and wireless charging. Prices start from Rs.2,250 for everyday TS buds and run to Rs.6,700 for the OS-3 — every pair works with both Android and iPhone.
