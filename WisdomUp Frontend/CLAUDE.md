# Nav rule: one fixed bar at a time (decided 2026-10-05)
- A page may mark ONE sticky bar with `data-priority-bar` (today: All Products filter bar `#mbar`, product page section tabs `#pdtabs`). It outranks the site nav: while the bar is stuck at the top the nav stays hidden even on scroll-up; the nav returns only once the bar is back in its normal place with room for the nav above it (logic in `placeNav()` in `common.js`). The product tabs' stuck `top` is its own (16px / 10px phone); the All Products filter bar is not sticky at all — it is the priority bar by position, and its floating twin takes the nav's spot — never offset for the nav.
- Nav size (Ronin-style, measured on ronin.pk 2026-10-05): desktop/tablet pill 56px tall (40px row + 8px padding, 0 22px 0 28px), 22px inside the hero; category buttons 40px tall with 24px icons and 4px gaps; Shop All 40px pill, 14px text; search/account/wishlist/cart 34px buttons with 22px icons; logo 30px tall. Phone nav stays 43px. Content under a non-hero nav starts at `.nav-spacer` 96px.
- Nav skin (decided 2026-10-06): smoked frosted glass — dark tint `rgba(22,18,17,.64)` (lighter `rgba(86,78,74,.30)` over dark banners via `.on-dark`), 30px blur, hair-thin 0.5px edge `rgba(226,232,255,.12)` (`::before`, taken from ClickUp Brain² on 2026-10-06) with a soft cool inset glow, fine grain (`::after`), soft sheen. Inner pills (Shop All, current category) use the same 0.5px hairline. Icons and logo are ALWAYS white; never bring back dark icons on light pages. Styles in `site/css/home.css` (`.site-nav`).
- Nav edge light (requested 2026-10-06, approved exception to the subtle-motion rule): one rainbow arc glides clockwise round the nav's 1px edge — speed, length and colours copied from the "Memory Updated" card on clickup.com/lp/brain: one lap per 4s on average, lit arc ≈ 40% of the edge (short orange head `#ed5f00` → magenta `#ff02f0` → blue `#0091ff` → fade-out tail). `.wu-edge` (1px ring mask) is painted each frame by `runEdges()` in common.js, which places the colours by distance along the pill outline (a plain conic gradient would race across a long bar). SPEED is elastic but forward-only (2026-10-06: the first spring version overshot and bounced — the user found it too aggressive; never let the light run backwards or bounce): a steady drift carries 35% of the pace and a soft pull every 2s the rest, smoothed by a low-pass plus a critically damped spring (`EDGE_FREQ` 4 rad/s, `EDGE_SOFT` 5/s, `EDGE_SHARE` .65), so the speed swells to ~1.9× the average and relaxes to ~0.4×. Speed only — never the colours/length/line. Same motion on the nav and the floating filter capsule. Pauses while the nav is hidden; off for prefers-reduced-motion.
- All Products header layout (requested 2026-10-06): one centred stack — small muted breadcrumb → big title → one-line blurb with "read more" → the centred filter capsule — then the grid toolbar: item count on the LEFT, Sort (and the phone 1/2-per-row toggle) on the RIGHT; the sort menu opens from the right edge. No divider lines in this header.
- Filter capsule (requested 2026-10-06): the All Products filter bar is a capsule of chips, the same component as the product page's section tabs (`.pdtabs`), with no dropdown chevrons. IN THE PAGE (`.mbar`): the tabs' exact look and sizes — light grey glass capsule 48px tall (8px padding, 4px gaps), 32px chips, 12px/600 text, 13px side padding, a set filter = white pill. It is NOT sticky — it scrolls away with the page and must never snap or pin. FLOATING TWIN (`.mfloat`, a separate element right after `.mbar`): once the capsule is fully out of view plus ~48px more (`.mbar.is-stuck`, set in `placeNav()`), the twin drops in smoothly from above the screen (560ms) to the nav's spot in the nav's skin and height (56px at 16px; phones 43px at 10px; dark glass, 0.5px hairline, edge light via `data-edge`); scrolling back it slides out upward (320ms) just before the original returns. The twin's chips are copies made by `syncFloat()` in products.js that pass clicks to the real chips (no ids/ARIA, never focusable, `aria-hidden`).
- Home page order (2026-10-06): hero → quick chips → carousel 1 → New for 2026 rail → creator/live duo → promos → Best Sellers rail → carousel 2 → Under Rs.1,000 rail → carousel 3 → Under Rs.2,000 rail → TS series → feature carousel `.feat` ("Built around your day": one big photo card 100vw−208px × 400px centred with 104px peeks, 24px gaps, heavy uppercase title + one line, two 34px black round buttons 16px below; narrow screens 72vw × ≤560px; copied from zennioptical.com/b/featherlite "Customize your lightweight look"; slides in `FEATS` in home.js, facts only; a 4th item = a video path under `site/video/` makes that card full-bleed video — muted, looped, plays only on screen, poster = product photo, never for prefers-reduced-motion) → Order in bulk → … The three big carousels share the same five slides but start on different ones (offsets 0/2/4).
- A back-to-top button (`.totop`) shows bottom-left on every page once you scroll more than ~80% of a screen down (above the product buy bar). Pages without a priority bar keep the normal hide-on-scroll-down / show-on-scroll-up nav.

# Control geometry follows the nav (decided 2026-10-05)
- Touch screens / phones hide left-right arrows (hero, rail steps, product gallery) — EXCEPT the home "loop" carousel's prev/next buttons beside its pause button, which stay on every screen (requested 2026-10-06).
- Favicon (2026-10-06): the diamond mark only (no wordmark), drawn as `site/img/favicon.svg` with `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `img/apple-touch-icon.png` (180, white) and `img/icon-512.png`; every page links all three. Regenerate from the logo if the mark changes.
- `site/css/controls.css` (linked LAST on every page) is the single source of control sizes. Every button, chip, tab, option picker, filter pill and arrow is 40px tall, pill-shaped, 14px semibold text (+.01em tracking), 16px side padding, ~22px icons; small tools (close ✕, rail arrows beside "Shop all") are 34px; bars that hold controls (filter bar, product tabs, search, newsletter) are 56px = 40 + 8px padding. Gaps: 4px tight groups, 8px chip rows, 11px between groups. Chips: soft #F1F2F4 fill + hairline, navy fill when selected.
- Same sizes on phones. Banners and product cards (incl. their internal buttons) keep their own larger sizes — don't touch them for control consistency. New controls must use the `--ctl` / `--ctl-s` / `--bar` / `--gap-*` tokens, never ad-hoc heights.

# Motion (decided 2026-10-06)
- `site/css/motion.css` (linked after controls.css) + `initMotion()` in common.js. Keep it very subtle: scroll reveal = 8px rise + fade 640ms (headings also un-blur 2px), siblings stagger 60ms (max 4); hover = 1px lift (cards 2px), press = scale .98. Never larger/faster/bouncier. Off for prefers-reduced-motion; content is only hidden when JS adds `.wu-motion`, and a position sweep guarantees nothing stays invisible.

# Cart, checkout, orders, reviews (decided 2026-10-05)
- Shop settings (delivery fees, free-delivery threshold, gift wrap, payment methods + account details, WhatsApp number, order statuses) live ONLY in `site/js/shop.js`, read by both the site and the order server. Never hard-code fees elsewhere.
- Orders: `api/orders.py` (Python, no deps) is the Vercel function AND is imported by `site/serve.py` locally — one implementation. It recomputes every price from `catalog.js` (never trust the browser), validates PK mobile numbers, and stores orders in Upstash Redis (env `KV_REST_API_URL`/`KV_REST_API_TOKEN` or `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_*`); locally in `.data/orders/` (gitignored). Admin = `site/admin.html` with header key `ADMIN_KEY` (local default `local-admin`). Tracking needs order number + the phone used.
- Payment: Cash on Delivery + manual JazzCash / EasyPaisa / bank transfer (receipt on WhatsApp). No card gateway — don't advertise Visa/MasterCard.
- Cart = sku + qty lines in localStorage `wu-cart` (`WU.add(id, qty, sku)`); cart panel slides from the right. Reviews = `WU.reviews` (device-only `wu-reviews` until a shared review API exists); stars show ONLY when a product has real reviews; "Verified buyer" = product in an order placed from this device.

# Catalogue (decided 2026-10-05)
- Products come ONLY from `site/js/catalog.js`, generated by `python3 tools/build_catalog.py` from the two supplier quote sheets (`智慧至上…2026.5.31.xlsx` in the parent folder). Never hand-edit catalog.js or wu-data product lists; change `tools/catalog_rules.py` and re-run (photos are cached in `site/img/p/`).
- Pakistan-only retail site, English only (the build fails if any Chinese text would reach the site). Price = sheet RMB × 4 × 45, whole rupees. US/Argentina plug chargers are not sold; rows without a price are skipped. Never show carton sizes, weights, barcodes, pieces-per-pack or RMB.
- Structure: 8 departments → product types → one product per family. Variants that differ only by Plug (EU 2-pin / UK 3-pin), Cable, Connector, Capacity or Length are ONE product with option chips; price/photo/model update with the choice.
- No invented data: no fake ratings/review counts, discounts or colour swatches — only what the sheets say.

# SEO (set up 2026-10-06 — full detail and keyword research in `SEO-REPORT.md`)
- Live address = `siteUrl` in `site/js/shop.js` (currently `https://wisdomup.pk/`). After changing it, adding/renaming a page, or regenerating pages: run `python3 tools/build_seo.py`. It rewrites the `<!-- SEO:start … SEO:end -->` head block in every `site/*.html` (title, description, robots, canonical, Open Graph/Twitter, JSON-LD) and regenerates `site/sitemap.xml` + `site/robots.txt`. Never hand-edit those blocks; add new pages to `PAGES` in that script (it refuses to run if one is missing).
- Pages built from the catalogue describe themselves in the browser with `WU.seo({title, description, path, image, type, ld})` (common.js): listing (`products.js`), product (`product.js`), blog post (`info.js`). Category copy (SEO name, intro, FAQs) lives in `site/js/seo-content.js`; `{n}`, `{min}`, `{max}`, `{year}` are filled from the live catalogue. Facts only — every product claim must match `catalog.js`; no ratings, discounts or superlatives.
- Title patterns: category `‹Name› Price in Pakistan ‹year› | WisdomUp` (H1 `‹Name› Price in Pakistan`); department `‹Name› in Pakistan — Prices ‹year› | WisdomUp`; product `‹Title› Price in Pakistan | WisdomUp`. One H1 per page.
- Addresses must be crawlable: categories are `products.html?cat=‹type›`, blog posts `article.html?p=‹slug›` (old `#…` links still resolve). Filter/sort combinations keep the plain category as canonical.
- SEO copy blocks are fold-outs ("Read more"), never text hidden from visitors (Google's hidden-text policy).
- Meta Pixel: off until `facebookPixelId` in `shop.js` is set. Events go through `WU.px()` — PageView, ViewContent, Search, AddToCart, AddToWishlist, InitiateCheckout, Purchase (once per order), Lead. Never send customer details.
- `vercel.json`: serves `site/` from the top level (canonicals use the short addresses) and sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on `demowisdomup.vercel.app` so the demo is never indexed.
